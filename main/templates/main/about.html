Our Story

Diamond Hill Resort stands as a beacon of luxury and comfort in the heart of Nagarkot, Nepal. Established with a vision to provide world-class hospitality, we have been welcoming guests from around the globe to experience the breathtaking beauty of the Himalayas.

Our resort is more than just a place to stay—it's a destination where memories are created, cultures blend, and nature's majesty takes center stage.

Whether you're seeking a romantic getaway, a family vacation, or a corporate retreat, Diamond Hill Resort offers the perfect setting for your unforgettable Himalayan experience.
